# Privacy

Sada has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no server of its own. It reads only what you ask it to check (the
page in your tab and public files of the same website) and analyses it in your browser. Nothing is sent to AYKA
TECHNOLOGIES or any third party. Settings, results and drafts stay in Chrome's extension storage on your computer and
are deleted with the extension.

Full policy (English, French, Arabic): https://getayka.com/sada/privacy/
